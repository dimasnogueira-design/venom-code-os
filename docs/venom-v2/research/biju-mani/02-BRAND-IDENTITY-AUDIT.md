# BIJU MANI — BRAND IDENTITY AUDIT 0.1
Date: 2026-10-08
Scope: public website + publicly indexed social/press/store imagery

## Confirmed public brand signals

Official Instagram:
@bijumanisp

Current positioning:
- Sorveteria Brasileira / Brazilian Ice Cream Co.
- Brasilidade as product philosophy, not decoration.
- Brazilian ingredients and local producers.
- Sustainability and bio-packaging.
- Appia as flagship competition flavor.
- National champion 2025.
- Global Top 10 in Gelato Festival World Masters 2026.
- Two São Paulo stores: Cidade São Francisco and Pinheiros.

Observed public storefront/packaging language:
- Biju Mani wordmark is used prominently on storefront and cups.
- "Brazilian Ice Cream Co." and "Since 2022" appear in current public storefront imagery.
- Physical identity uses stronger, warmer, more saturated color than a generic luxury gelateria.
- Product photography is an important brand asset and should remain central.

## Identity principle to preserve

CINEMA INTERNACIONAL COM ALMA VISUAL BRASILEIRA.

Meaning:
Global production value, Brazilian visual/product soul.

The redesign must NOT:
- turn the brand into an Italian gelateria;
- erase color in favor of generic beige luxury;
- make the brand dark/black everywhere;
- introduce unrelated tropical clichés;
- replace product truth with decorative effects.

The redesign SHOULD:
- use cinematic lighting and macro food photography;
- derive color from actual ingredients and current brand/store language;
- preserve the existing logo/wordmark unless client authorizes brand redesign;
- make Appia, Brazilian ingredients and award proof central;
- use contemporary Brazilian art-direction rather than flag-color literalism.

## Toucan status

A toucan/avian mascot was mentioned by the client-side contact, but this element is NOT YET VERIFIED from a directly accessible official brand asset in this audit.

Do not invent, redraw, animate or make it a major web element until we obtain:
- official logo/brand manual;
- vector/logo package;
- packaging file;
- or a clear official social post/profile asset confirming its role.

Once confirmed, classify it as one of:
A. primary logo mark;
B. secondary brand character;
C. packaging illustration;
D. campaign-only graphic.

Its web use depends on that classification.

## Social/content implications

Instagram and press currently carry fresher brand reality than the website:
- Pinheiros is active;
- Top 10 world result has happened;
- current delivery ecosystem includes multiple platforms;
- current opening hours differ from old site copy.

Therefore the new site should not treat the existing website as the only source of truth.

## Required asset request before visual lock

Ask the brand owner for:
1. logo in SVG/PDF/AI;
2. any toucan/illustration master files;
3. official color palette;
4. brand fonts/licenses;
5. packaging artwork;
6. store photography;
7. product photography in original resolution;
8. current menu/flavor list;
9. award photos/press permissions;
10. current store addresses/hours/delivery links.

## Current creative direction

Hero:
cinematic macro of Appia, dark-warm opening, then expansion into the brand's real colors.

Color:
ingredient-led, not invented:
cream / honey / caramel / citrus / fruit / leaf tones, reconciled with official palette once received.

Motion:
slow, tactile, food-first:
liquid flow, ingredient assembly, masks, parallax, product reveals.

Typography:
editorial premium + clear functional sans.
Do not replace official identity typography blindly; audit license and current usage first.

## Decision gate

No final Design System should be locked until official logo/toucan/color/font assets are obtained or visually verified from first-party material.
