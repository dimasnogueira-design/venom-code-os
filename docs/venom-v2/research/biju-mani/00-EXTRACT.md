## 1. What the current site communicates

Primary proposition:
- Brazilian ice cream / gelato with a strong brasilidade thesis.
- Local Brazilian ingredients.
- Quality + sustainability.
- Award-winning Appia flavor.
- Delivery + physical store.

Brand story:
- "Biju" references traditional biju/casquinha imagery.
- "Mani" references mani-oca / mandioca and indigenous/Brazilian origin.
- Founder story centers Guilherme Geraldini, gastronomy and Brazilian ingredients.

Current content architecture:
- Home
- Location
- Contact
- About / Biju Mani story
- Delivery CTAs
- Long-form manifesto/pillars

## 2. Strongest brand assets

### A. Competitive proof
The brand is much stronger than the current website presentation:
- Brazilian winner in Gelato Festival World Masters national final 2025.
- Appia as flagship award flavor.
- 10th place in Gelato Festival World Masters 2026.
- First Brazilian top-10 placement reported by major food press.
- Two current São Paulo locations, including Pinheiros.

### B. Signature product
Appia is not just a menu item. It is the narrative anchor:
- artisanal wheat/honey beer;
- uruçu honey / Brazilian honey story;
- lime;
- macadamia;
- salted caramel;
- Ribeirão Preto / founder origin;
- competition proof.

### C. Brasilidade
The most ownable strategic territory:
"Brazilian ice cream" rather than imitation Italian gelato language.

### D. Ingredient narrative
Cumaru, jabuticaba, caju, ginger, Brazilian classics and local suppliers create a rich visual/editorial system.

## 3. Current-site problems

### Content freshness — P0
The home still frames the 2026 world final as a future event.
By October 2026 that story is obsolete.
The brand has already reached the global top 10.

### Location freshness — P0
The public site prominently exposes the Cidade São Francisco location.
Current press and listings show a second Pinheiros unit that should be first-class site content.

### Hierarchy — P0
The largest proof point competes with repeated paragraphs.
Award, Appia, brasilidade, store visit and delivery are not separated into a clear conversion narrative.

### Repetition — P1
"Novo conceito em Sorveteria Brasileira", award copy and brasilidade claims repeat instead of progressing the story.

### Long-form readability — P1
Important narrative is delivered as long blocks.
The founder story and brand etymology deserve editorial pacing, not one continuous body.

### Premium perception — P1
The company has world-class proof but the site does not visually signal world-class gastronomy.

### Conversion structure — P1
Delivery and store CTAs exist but are not organized around user intent:
- visit a store;
- see flavors;
- order delivery;
- understand the award;
- follow launches.

## 4. Platform clues

Public assets are served from assets.zyrosite.com.
This is consistent with a Hostinger/Zyro website-builder stack.

Confidence: STRONG SIGNAL.

Implication:
The current site is easy to maintain, but its visual and interaction ceiling is lower than the brand's current positioning.

## 5. What should be preserved

Do not erase:
- brasilidade;
- Biju/Mani naming story;
- ingredient provenance;
- sustainability;
- founder credibility;
- playful food photography;
- direct store/delivery utility.

Do not turn Biju Mani into a generic beige luxury gelateria.

## 6. What should be elevated

From:
"sorveteria artesanal with award banner"

To:
"Brazilian gastronomic brand that placed Brazil in the world gelato top 10."

The design should make Brazilian ingredient culture feel contemporary, desirable and globally competitive.
