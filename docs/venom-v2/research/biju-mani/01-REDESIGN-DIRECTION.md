# BIJU MANI — PREMIUM REDESIGN DIRECTION 0.1

## Creative thesis

BRAZILIAN ICE CREAM, WORLD-CLASS.

Not Italian cosplay.
Not tropical cliché.
Not rustic craft-market aesthetic.

The site should feel like contemporary Brazilian gastronomy:
warm, tactile, authored, cultural and internationally credible.

## Hero concept

Full-bleed macro photography/video of Appia.
Texture first: cream, caramel, macadamia, citrus zest.

Editorial type:
TOP 10 DO MUNDO
SORVETE BRASILEIRO.

Secondary line:
Appia levou ingredientes e memória brasileira ao Gelato Festival World Masters 2026.

CTA:
Conheça o Appia
Visite uma loja

## Visual language

Palette should be derived from real product/store photography, not invented upfront:
- cream;
- caramel/honey;
- dark cocoa/coffee;
- leaf/cumaru greens;
- fruit accents.

Use Brazilian color through ingredients rather than flag colors.

## Typography

Editorial display serif or expressive grotesk + restrained sans-serif.
The identity should feel gastronomic/cultural, not tech.

Need:
- strong large headlines;
- compact labels;
- short body paragraphs;
- ingredient captions.

## Motion

Subtle food cinematography:
- slow macro parallax;
- ingredient reveals;
- text masks;
- horizontal flavor strip;
- gentle depth.

No gratuitous WebGL.
No tech particles.
No Venom visual language.

## Signature interaction — APPPIA COMPOSITION

Interactive ingredient composition:
APPIA
Beer / honey / lime / macadamia / salted caramel

As user moves/scrolls, ingredient layers assemble around the scoop.
Could be achieved with DOM/video/masked images, not necessarily 3D.

## Brasilidade chapter

Avoid generic Brazil iconography.

Show:
- ingredient names;
- places;
- producers;
- origin maps;
- short cultural notes;
- seasonal flavors.

Make Brazilian ingredients the interface.

## Stores

Two-location experience:
Pinheiros
Cidade São Francisco

Each:
- image;
- address;
- opening hours;
- map/directions;
- delivery options.

## Press / proof

Use text-based press citations / approved logos:
- national champion 2025;
- world top 10 2026;
- competition chronology.

Proof should appear early.

## Mobile

Food-first.
Fast.
No intro animation.
Sticky bottom actions:
LOJAS / DELIVERY

## Commercial positioning

This redesign should feel like a brand that can:
- expand locations;
- collaborate with chefs/brands;
- sell limited drops;
- attract press;
- support future ecommerce;
- present itself internationally.

## Potential Venom case-study angle

Problem:
world-class product, under-positioned digital presence.

Intervention:
strategy + content architecture + art direction + web experience.

Outcome target:
digital perception aligned with actual gastronomic authority.
